Behind the work

An archive with the energy of the artwork.

One site. Several visual identities. A campaign archive for independent nights in Agra, with the original posters setting the pace.

The poster leads.

The HEAT artwork establishes the red and black palette. The homepage places it beside a rendered sculpture; individual campaign pages give the original artwork a quieter, more readable setting.

One source, several views.

Typed campaign records supply the homepage previews, archive and event routes. Each record carries its own artwork dimensions and source label, distinguishing original posters from frames extracted from supplied footage.

Motion with an exit.

The sculpture is a locally rendered React Three Fiber scene. Its component also provides a still fallback. Event footage uses native controls and waits for the visitor to play it.

What this study shows

Captured from the local website. Campaign identities and artwork sources were checked against the project’s campaign records. No attendance, booking or business results are claimed.

The visual source

From poster to page.

The supplied HEAT artwork sets the colour, density and tone. The website gives that identity a setting without redrawing the original poster.
