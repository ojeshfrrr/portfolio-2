Behind the work

Identity, information and moving image.

A digital experience for Teen Fiesta, bringing its visual identity, event information and available media together in one cohesive website.

The film has its own space.

The verified film section combines expressive typography, a short introduction and an embedded player. It gives the moving image a clear place in the page rather than starting playback behind the interface.

Use the material that exists.

The supporting sequence contains visibly Teen Fiesta-branded supplied artwork and an announcement. These are presented as supplied media, separate from the website captures and the development work.

Playback stays in the browser.

The local implementation uses a video element with native controls after activation. It includes loading and retry states and pauses playback when the film leaves the viewport.

Local presentation

Presented from the existing local website export and verified supplied Teen Fiesta media. The public deployment is unavailable. This is not presented as a live public deployment, a ticketing integration or an account of event results.
