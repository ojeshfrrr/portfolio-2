Only the current/selected large image is requested; thumbnails are small/lazy. Natural aspect ratios replace the old landscape height cap. Runtime quality 90 is explicitly allowed by Next Image.

## Defects found during review

1. The original case introduction/gallery cap wasted much of the first viewport. Headers were tightened, metadata moved below the gallery, and artwork now precedes captions/controls. Before/after screenshots use matching **1440×900** dimensions.
2. The first entry recording exposed **two copies of the work during handoff**: the destination was uncovered before the moving surface arrived. The final implementation holds the destination covered until alignment. Four per-frame tests sampled **601 frames**, with **zero uncovered moving frames**. Native wheel, keyboard or pointer input cancels the handoff instead of targeting stale geometry.
3. The first film test hit the native timeline rather than Play. Its failed result is retained in `production/test-results-first-run.json`. The actual play button was identified in `film-before.png`, clicked, and playback verified (16.415s duration, advancing current time, readyState 4). No playback workaround or application change was needed for that test correction.

The initial double-image recording/filmstrip are retained with `before-alignment-fix` filenames. The final recording and alignment measurements supersede them.
